# Copyright Research

> [!danger] Not legal advice
> Nothing in this vault, and nothing the software produces, is legal advice or a
> clearance. See [[Never Claim Legal Safety]].

## Positions taken

**Commentary is not the problem.** A documentary *about* a franchise is ordinary.
Reusing that franchise's own artwork, card scans, screenshots or broadcast
footage is a different act.

**A licence covers the photograph, not what it is a photograph of.** A permissive
licence on someone's picture of a trading card does not license the card.

**Attribution is not optional.** Anything whose licence requires a credit carries
that credit into the licence report and the description. Dropping one is a breach.

**Unrecognised means attribution required.** Guessing permissively is a breach;
guessing strictly costs a credit line.
