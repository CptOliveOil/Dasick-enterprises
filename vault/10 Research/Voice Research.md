# Voice Research

**Duration must be measured, not estimated.** Probing the returned audio is the
only honest source; a words-per-minute estimate drifts and every downstream
timing inherits it.

**155 words per minute** is a reasonable documentary pace and is what the script
estimator uses — but it is only ever an estimate until the audio exists.

**Word timings would remove local caption drift.** Fitting cues to the total
duration removes *accumulated* drift but cannot fix a pause the narrator took
that the script did not predict. See [[Subtitles]].
