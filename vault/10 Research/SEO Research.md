# SEO Research

**Chapters must start at 00:00** or the platform ignores the entire list. The
publisher inserts an `Introduction` chapter if the first is later.

**Under 60 characters** for a title survives truncation in search and on mobile.

**Descriptions carry the attribution lines** required by asset licences. That is
a licence obligation, not an SEO choice.

**Nothing here is measured yet.** Once [[Business Memory]] has click-through
figures for this channel they replace these conventions.
