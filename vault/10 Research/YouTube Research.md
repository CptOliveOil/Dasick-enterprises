# YouTube Research

> [!warning] Confidence
> Everything here is **belief**, not measurement. Once [[Business Memory]] has
> real figures for this channel, they replace these notes. A belief that survives
> contact with data becomes a note in the business page; one that does not is
> deleted.

## Working assumptions

| Belief | Confidence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold opens should raise a question in the first 15 seconds | medium | craft convention |
| Channel branding in the first 30 seconds costs retention | medium | craft convention |
| Chapters must start at 00:00 or are ignored entirely | **high** | platform documented |
| Titles under 60 characters survive truncation | **high** | measurable |
| Uploads default to private is safer than public | **high** | irreversibility |

## What we deliberately do not assume

- That any topic performs better than another. [[Business Memory]] will say.
- That thumbnails with faces outperform objects. Untested on this channel.
- That a particular length is optimal.
