# Islam App

> [!info] Goals
> Produce content where every religious claim carries a verifiable source, and nothing proceeds until each unresolved claim is settled.

## Agents

[[Islamic Researcher]] · [[Islamic Source Checker]], then the YouTube production workforce.

## Capabilities

`islamic.research` · `islamic.content_plan` · `islamic.source_verify` · `islamic.script_review`

## Workflows

[[Islamic YouTube Video]] — verification comes *before* the script, so a bad citation is caught early.

## Metrics

Engagement and reading patterns, once published.

## Business Memory

1. The app surface itself is not built — only the content pipeline
2. Source policy per channel exists; broader policy templates do not

## Roadmap

Per-business, like every other. Nothing is shared with the general channel.
